About me

I help organizations deliver less — but with greater impact.


For over 20 years I've been at the intersection of technology, product, and business value — from early project management roles at PricewaterhouseCoopers, through leading a banking BI portfolio at Ness Digital Engineering, to shaping enterprise Agile transformation at KPMG and Česká spořitelna, one of Central Europe's largest banks.

My work is grounded in a simple belief: most organizations invest heavily in delivery while neglecting the harder question — are we delivering the right things? I bridge that gap by designing cross-team prioritization systems, introducing backtesting of delivered initiatives against real production data, and connecting product decisions to measurable outcomes.

Product Management, for me, isn't a role — it's a way to manage value. Agile isn't a process — it's a way to learn faster. And data is what makes decisions real.

My approach

How I work

Every engagement follows the same discipline: understand reality first, design the right intervention, then deliver and validate it.

🔍

Diagnose

I start with the real problem — not the stated one. Through interviews, data review, and system observation I map where value is leaking and why.

🗺️

Design

Together we co-create a targeted intervention: a prioritization system, an experiment framework, a coaching programme — whatever the diagnosis calls for.

🚀

Deliver & Validate

I help implement the change, then measure whether it worked in production. Every recommendation I make is just a hypothesis until the data confirms it.
